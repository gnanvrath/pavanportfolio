01 — Profile

Hello, I'm Pavan.

I'm a Hyderabad-based, Telugu-speaking theatre actor, voice-over artist and RJ. I've been active in theatre since 2022 — training and performing with Samahaara before joining the theatre group Sifar in 2024, where I continue to work today.

Alongside stage work, I host and voice programming for All India Radio (Akashvani Hyderabad), including a regular RJ show on Rainbow FM 101.9, special national-day broadcasts, and voice-over work for AIR. I also host my own podcast and have appeared in television commercial and screen work.

I move comfortably between English, Telugu, Tamil and Hindi — on stage, on air, on mic, and on screen.
