01 — About

I chase the small, true moments — the ones an audience feels before they can name them.

Pavan Kannan is an actor working across theatre and film, with a home in Telugu stage and screen. His range moves easily between the raw intensity of a dramatic close-up and the timing a live stage demands, grounded always in a search for what feels real.

Trained through the stage and shaped by ensemble work, he brings a watchful, lived-in presence to every role — whether a brooding lead, a comic beat, or the still figure at the edge of the frame. Off the stage, he works as a radio jockey, an ear for voice and rhythm that feeds back into the work.
